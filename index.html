Who I am

Grounded in the workshop, growing in the classroom.

I started with a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering at Government Polytechnic Sakoli and am now building on that foundation as a second-year B.Tech Mechanical student.

My interest sits at the point where design meets the shop floor — turning a CAD drawing into a part that actually works. That pull led me to study electrical discharge machining closely and to spend time inside a real transport workshop, watching how maintenance and repair decisions get made under time pressure.

Outside the drafting table, I compete — I paired up to win a table tennis doubles match, which is the same instinct I bring to engineering: read the situation fast, adjust, and follow through.
